HOW IT BEGAN

Our Story

It started with a shared table at a mutual friend's birthday dinner — a conversation about nothing in particular that somehow lasted until the restaurant closed. Six years, two dogs, and one very small apartment later, Elias asked Nadia to marry him on the same stretch of beach where they had their first weekend trip together. She said yes before he finished the sentence.
