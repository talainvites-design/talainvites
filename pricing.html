Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What is the difference between the two packages?
The Invitation Proper is a single full-page wedding invitation with RSVP, gallery, entourage, and all the details your guests need. The Wedding Suite adds a Save the Date page — a beautifully designed teaser that goes out first, with a link to the full invitation when it's ready. Both are fully bespoke.
How long does it take to receive my invitation?
Standard turnaround is 48 hours for priority delivery. Rush options are available subject to schedule — please reach out first to confirm availability.
How many revisions are included?
Unlimited revisions are included within 7 days of your first draft. After that window, additional revision rounds are available as an add-on at ₱150 per round.
What happens when my link expires?
Your invitation link stays active for 2 years from the date it goes live. Link extensions are available as an add-on — just reach out before or after expiry and we'll sort it for you.
How do I pay and place an order?
Send us an inquiry through our contact form or message us via talainvites@gmail.com. We'll send a booking confirmation and payment details. We accept GCash, bank transfer, and PayMaya.
Will you offer other packages in the future?
Yes — we are building out additional tiers for birthdays, debuts, and other celebrations and will be launching them soon. For now, we're focused on delivering the best possible wedding invitation experience.
