Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does it take to receive my invitation?
Standard turnaround is 5 days for Pahiwatig, 3 days for Diwa, and 48 hours for Tala Suite. Rush delivery (24–48 hrs) is available as an add-on for Pahiwatig and Diwa, subject to availability.
Can I request a theme that isn't in the preset list?
Yes — fully custom color palettes are available as an add-on (₱300). Tala Suite includes fully bespoke design by default, so no add-on is needed at that tier.
What happens when my link expires?
Your invitation link will become inactive after the active period. You can extend it at any time — before or after expiry — using the Link Extension add-on. Just reach out and we'll sort it for you.
Do you offer a printed version of the invitation?
We currently specialize in web (digital) invitations only. A printer-ready PDF version of your design is available as an add-on (₱350), which you can then take to any print shop.
How do I pay and place an order?
Send us an inquiry through our contact form or via hello@talainvites.co, and we'll send you a booking form with payment details. We accept GCash, bank transfer, and PayMaya.

The Beginning

Every invitation is a first impression.

Tala Invites was founded in the Philippines with a single belief — that the announcement of a celebration should be as memorable as the celebration itself. That the first thing a guest receives should already feel like a gift.

We noticed that beautiful, thoughtful invitations were often out of reach for many families — not because of taste, but because of price. So we set out to change that. Starting from ₱500, every Tala invitation is handled with the same devotion to craft, beauty, and intention.

The name Tala is a Filipino word for star — for light, for clarity, for the kind of brightness that guides you somewhere special. That's what we hope every invitation becomes: a small, beautiful light that leads your guests toward a moment they'll always remember.

Timeless

Classical, not dated. Our designs draw from tradition and craft a language that will feel as elegant in ten years as it does today.

Elegant

Restrained, not cold. Every element earns its place. We believe that less, done beautifully, is always more.

Intimate

Personal, not generic. Warmth is embedded in every design. No two celebrations are the same, and no two invitations should be either.

Celebratory

Joyful, not boisterous. Every event deserves delight — quiet, refined delight that feels like a promise of something wonderful ahead.
