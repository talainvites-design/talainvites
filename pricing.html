Ask a Question

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does it take to receive my invitation?
Standard turnaround is 5 days for Pahiwatig, 3 days for Diwa, and 48 hours for Tala Suite. Rush delivery (24–48 hrs) is available as an add-on, subject to availability.
What does "Final upon submission" mean for Pahiwatig?
The Pahiwatig tier is finalized once your content is submitted and the invitation goes live. There are no revision rounds included — however, post-launch edits are available for ₱150 per change. We recommend reviewing all details carefully before submitting.
Can I request a theme that isn't in the preset list?
Yes — a fully custom color palette swap is available as an add-on (₱300). Tala Suite includes a fully bespoke design by default, so no add-on is needed at that tier.
What happens when my link expires?
Your invitation link will become inactive after the active period. You can extend it at any time — before or after expiry — using the Link Extension add-on. Just reach out and we'll sort it for you.
Do you offer a printed version of the invitation?
We currently specialize in web (digital) invitations only. A printer-ready PDF version of your design is available as an add-on (₱350), which you can take to any print shop.
How do I pay and place an order?
Send us an inquiry through our contact form or via talainvites@gmail.com. We'll send you a booking confirmation with payment details. We accept GCash, bank transfer, and PayMaya.
